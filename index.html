Do I need followers, an audience, or a face on camera?

No. The kit is built for faceless operation. Distribution covers off-search funnels (Pinterest, email, niche communities) precisely because you don't need a personal brand to reach people.

How long does this really take?

The 72 hours are a workflow target: three focused working days (build, list, promote). Most people finish inside it when they keep the scope small — something the kit forces on Day 1.

No. Nothing here promises income, traffic, or results. The kit gives you the workflow and the copy frameworks; outcomes depend on your product, audience, execution, and distribution.

No. Personal-use license: use the kit to build and sell products you create, but you may not resell, redistribute, or republish the kit itself.

Markdown so you can read and edit in any tool, plus HTML pages you can print or save to PDF. Planner formats: A4, US Letter, tablet-friendly.
